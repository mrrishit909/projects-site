# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and technical reviewers screening Rishit Raj Mathur for entry-level data and business analyst
roles (US-wide and remote). Situation: a few minutes between other candidates, often on a laptop, sometimes a phone.
Job: decide in under a minute whether the work is real and substantial, open one case study to check the depth, find the
résumé and a way to contact him. (Source: the owner's brief and BRIEF.md; audience confirmed in earlier sessions.)

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site (the home of mrrishit909.github.io/projects/) that shows the body of work (224 case studies,
219 public repositories, counts read from data/github.json at build time, never hard-coded), carries four flagship
projects in depth, links the résumé, GitHub, LinkedIn and e-mail, and gives fast searchable access to the whole archive
(all.html). Success: a visitor opens a flagship case study, reads it, downloads the résumé or makes contact.

## Positioning

Every number on the site was recomputed by a second, plainer script from its public source (BTS, FRED, SEC, Census, city
portals) before it was written down; each case study names its source and its check. The claim in one line: "Every
number, checked against its source." A neighbouring portfolio can show dashboards; it cannot truthfully claim the check.
(Source: build.py, the case studies' `scale.evidence` fields, BRIEF.md.)

## Operating Context

Static site generated by `python3 build.py` (standard library only) from `<slug>/project.json`, `featured.json` and
`data/github.json`; generated HTML is committed; published by GitHub Pages. Work goes to the private repository
mrrishit909/projects-site (remote `private`, branch `unified-portfolio`); the public site is updated only when the owner
says so. Vendored: Three.js r170 (tree-shaken), GSAP 3.13 + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, self-hosted fonts. Local checks:
`python3 -m http.server 8800`, `tests/portfolio-check.cjs` (Playwright), Lighthouse via `npx serve` on 8801.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Four flagships (featured.json): florida-flight-delays (Power BI star schema, 635,459 flights, 75.5% on time),
  retail-dbt (dbt on DuckDB, 31 tests over 1.07M invoice lines), us-retail-forecast (1.81% MAPE rolling-origin
  backtest), sec-10k-rag (retrieval over 10-K filings that cites its source). Each has a live motion scene
  (`motion/scenes/<slug>.js`) drawing its own interface from its own figures.
- Every case study has a cover.jpg, a loop video, a thumbnail and a generated page; the archive page is a real query.
- Professional claims come only from ~/portfolio/resume.pdf (USF REACH Lab research assistant, USF Athletics ticketing
  operations, TraceRT business analyst with the 22% report-turnaround figure, Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst Associate,
  B.S. Business Analytics and Information Systems May 2026, M.S. candidate expected May 2028). No phone number on the
  site (owner's privacy choice).
- Binding visual rules from the owner (recorded, not expanded here): loud colour; one dominant 3D object; huge type; little
  text; no blur of any kind; no thumbnail wall. No em dashes anywhere.
- Undecided: whether the public site is ever switched to this home (owner decides).

## Product Principles

1. A number is shown with the thing that proves it.
2. Depth on demand: one line per screen on the home, the full story in the case study.
3. The site works without WebGL, without JavaScript and with reduced motion; the spectacle is additive.
4. Nothing on the page is a claim the résumé or a repository cannot back.
5. The archive is fast and searchable; the home is edited to a few things.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target: semantic HTML, one h1, landmarks, skip link, visible focus, 44 px targets, reduced-motion composition,
canvas never the only content model. Measured with the project's Playwright suite and Lighthouse.
