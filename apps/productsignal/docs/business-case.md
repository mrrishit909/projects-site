# ProductSignal: business case (P06-ART-01)

**Every stakeholder, scenario and number here is synthetic.** Nothing is a claim about a real product, a real customer, a real experiment or a measured benefit (P06-GOAL-12, P06-DOMAIN-05).
Dataset `ps-20261026-20261026` (seed 20261026, generator `dataset_hash` `12672f9c39402517083bbbbe9164cbefe3435361dff086eb0d9e16e60f9b66f8`), contract v1, code revision `fdf911a`; the observed figures describe the event log at the as-of **2026-10-05T00:00:00Z** (26 weeks from 2026-04-06, 600 accounts, 1,824 users, 12,552 event rows) and the five logged analysis runs of one onboarding experiment in two versions.
Three kinds of statement are kept apart throughout: **observed** (what the synthetic history shows, reconciled to `data/sql/reference_metrics.sql` and the Python domain), **constructed** (what the seeded generator built in on purpose: a designed activation effect of +8.00 percentage points, a sample-ratio mismatch in v2, an injected instrumentation gap, and four choices its author made after looking at earlier drafts of the data) and **hypothetical** (what a real product team might gain; none is claimed and no figure below is one).

**How to read it:** this file needs nothing installed. The numbers are quoted from `sql-analysis.md` (observed, investigations 01-08 and the reconciliation of its section 9); `requirements.md` traces each requirement id to code and tests; `executive-memo.md` turns the evidence into a recommendation. Proportions are decimal fractions with six places, lifts are percentage points (pp), money is USD per assigned account, and every rate names its denominator.

## The business decision
**Should an onboarding change ship, stop, or receive more investigation?** (P06-GOAL-01.) The primary user is a product analyst, with the experimentation analyst and the data analyst as the other portfolio roles (P06-GOAL-02). The product never ships anything: a decision recorded by C10 `record-decision` writes no product state (P06-FLOW-10).

## Business problem
A product team ran an onboarding checklist experiment and argues about a dashboard. The failure modes the generator plants on purpose (twelve injected defects, `data/injection-manifest.json`) are the ones that make such a review wrong: users are counted where accounts were randomised; the result is read on day 10 of a 28-day plan and called final; a sample-ratio mismatch nobody checked sits under the lift; a cohort that signed up three days ago shows a seven-day activation rate; a segment that happened to look good is reported as the finding; and retention is one number that means exact day 7 on one page and "any time after" on another.
ProductSignal fixes the measurement first: one account-level unit, intention to treat with the exposure rate beside it, the sample ratio checked before any effect is shown, a plan frozen before any outcome is read with every analysis run logged as interim or final, cohorts that only mature when their window is complete, retention on exact calendar days in the account's timezone, segments labelled exploratory with their multiplicity, a cutoff rule under which nothing dated after a run's cutoff counts, and a decision that is ship, stop or inconclusive.

What the synthetic history shows (OBSERVED, as-of 2026-10-05T00:00:00Z; `sql-analysis.md`):
* **The population:** 600 accounts (12 internal and never counted), 588 of them with a signup; **571 are mature** (seven days complete) and 17 are not, so the seven-day activation rate over all mature signups is **0.374781** (214 of 571). 12,552 event rows: 124 re-sent duplicates dropped, 30 of unknown identity, 305 of bots and 114 of internal accounts excluded, **11,979 analysed**; 237 rows (0.019070) arrived more than 24 hours late; 0.697121 of the real users are reached by an identity link.
* **v1 (the experiment, 400 accounts in permuted blocks of four, plan frozen 2026-04-05T12:00:00Z, planned end 2026-08-02):** three interim runs (2026-05-18, 2026-06-15, 2026-07-13) and a final run at planned_end. The interim runs show +11.37, +11.91 and +9.15 points and none excludes zero (the first sees only the 155 accounts assigned by then, 77 and 78). **The final run reads 63 of 200 control against 82 of 200 treatment accounts activated within seven days: +9.50 pp, relative lift 0.301587, Newcombe 95% interval +0.000812 to +0.186863 (+0.08 to +18.69 pp), the sample ratio 200 / 200 OK: conclusive by a hair.**
* **The published v1 lift sits inside an instrumentation gap (INJ-10, R6-08):** `activated` was not instrumented for signups before 2026-04-08, which reaches 11 mature v1 accounts (5 control, 6 treatment). **Published +9.50 pp, drawn +11.50 pp (a constructed figure: the generator knows the 4 suppressed activations), +9.96 pp excluding the gap accounts** (interval +0.35 to +19.32). The domain cannot see the gap; it is disclosed beside "conclusive by a hair", not corrected.
* **The guardrail and the other outcomes do not decide it:** the error rate is 31 of 200 against 30 of 200 (-0.50 pp, interval -0.076005 to +0.066018, verdict OK); week-4 retention is 71 of 200 against 62 of 200 (-4.50 pp, interval -0.136053 to +0.047102, includes zero); revenue per assigned account is USD 97.83 against USD 130.95 (difference 33.12, Welch interval -25.59 to +91.83, includes zero).
* **v2 (a re-plan after v1, 154 accounts planned 0.60 / 0.40) has a sample-ratio mismatch:** 62 control and 92 treatment accounts, chi-square 25.004329, p below 0.000001. The mismatch is shown in place of a lift and blocks both ship and stop; only `inconclusive` can be recorded.

## Scope
In scope: a canonical event log (received by the cutoff, deduplicated, bots and internal accounts excluded, identity forward only); the account as the unit of randomisation and retention; a versioned funnel in strict order with a maximum gap; seven-day activation with maturity; day-7, on-or-after-day-7 and week-4 retention on the account's own calendar; feature adoption; intention to treat with the exposure rate, a chi-square sample-ratio check, Wilson and Newcombe intervals, relative lift that is null when the control is zero; a frozen analysis plan with a hash; interim and final runs; the guardrail error rate and revenue per assigned account with intervals; exploratory segments with a Bonferroni threshold; a cutoff rule (D-08) and a decision that is ship, stop or inconclusive.
Out of scope: shipping anything (a decision writes no product state), causal claims from observational feature correlations, an automatic release, a chatbot or any paid inference (P06-GOAL-11), live customer data and any operational write from the public site (P06-GOAL-12).

## Decision criteria
1. **The sample ratio comes first.** A chi-square test of the assigned counts against the plan's allocation: MISMATCH when p < 0.001, INSUFFICIENT when an expected cell is below 5, else OK. A mismatch is shown in place of a lift and blocks `ship` and `stop` (P06-DOMAIN-04).
2. **The plan is frozen before any outcome is read** (primary outcome activation within seven days, the guardrail, the horizon and the allocation, with a sha256 `config_hash`); every run cites the hash and the number of earlier runs (P06-DOMAIN-03).
3. **Intention to treat on the account.** Every assigned mature account counts in its assigned arm whether or not it was exposed; the exposure rate (0.875000 and 0.870000) is reported beside it (P06-DOMAIN-01, 02). An activation counts only at or after the assignment.
4. **Conclusive is a rule, not a feeling (D-10).** A result is conclusive only when the Newcombe 95% interval excludes 0 **and** the run is final (cutoff at or after planned_end) **and** the plan hash is valid **and** the plan was frozen before the first logged run **and** the sample ratio is OK. An interim run is never conclusive and never supports a ship.
5. **A harmful guardrail blocks a ship.** HARMFUL means the whole interval of the treatment-minus-control error-rate difference lies above 0; an interval that includes 0 is "no harm shown", not "no harm".
6. **Secondary outcomes are read, not decided on.** Week-4 retention and revenue per account are reported with their intervals; a difference whose interval includes zero is neither a benefit nor a harm.
7. **Segments are exploratory** with the number tested and a Bonferroni threshold; no decision cites one (P06-DOMAIN-06).
8. **Data-quality caveats travel with the figure.** The INJ-10 gap is stated beside "conclusive by a hair" with all three numbers (published +9.50, drawn +11.50, +9.96 excluding the gap accounts); outcome-informed generator choices are listed wherever the result is quoted.
9. **A run counts only what existed by its cutoff (D-08).** Assignments, exposures, subscriptions and events dated or received after the cutoff are not in it; a later run includes them as a disclosed revision, never a rewrite.

## Decision supported
The memo (`executive-memo.md`) applies these criteria to the synthetic experiment: **for v1, the pre-specified rule is met by a hair** (final run an-004, the plan frozen first, the sample ratio OK, the interval +0.000812 to +0.186863, no harmful guardrail), so a `ship` is permitted by C10; the recommendation is a staged ship with the weakness of the evidence stated and a follow-up read, not a claim of a large effect. **For v2, the only decision the rules allow is `inconclusive`** (a mismatch blocks both ship and stop); v2 cannot confirm or contradict v1. That is a recommendation about a synthetic experiment; the hypothetical real-world benefit of shipping is not claimed.

## Limits and honesty
* **The history is generated, in one draw.** The designed relationships were chosen, so finding them shows the queries work, not that a product has them. Four generator choices were made after seeing earlier drafts and are disclosed in the manifest: v1's randomisation was changed from a fair coin (223 / 177, p 0.021) to permuted blocks; `product_error` was changed from per-account to 8% per session after an unrealistic guardrail gap appeared; v2's enrolment start and the number of bounce visitors were tuned; the INJ-06 candidate window was widened. Everything else is reported as drawn, once.
* **"Conclusive by a hair" is the lower bound +0.000812** (+0.08 points) of one interval, and it sits inside a known instrumentation gap; the result does not say how large the effect is, only that zero is barely outside the interval.
* **The generator's v1 passes the sample-ratio check by construction** (permuted blocks) and v2 fails it by construction (3 of every 5 accounts to treatment); neither is a finding about a randomiser.
* **No decision exists in the data.** The snapshot database holds no `decisions`, `guardrail_reviews` or `audit_events` row; they are produced by C09 and C10 through the API, and the decision screen of the public site simulates them in browser memory.
* **Mode A and Mode B are different evidence.** The transactional behaviour is verified on hosted runners against PostgreSQL; the public site serves a published snapshot and simulates its actions.
* **No Excel, no Power BI.** The workbook (`workbook.md`) was recalculated by LibreOffice only; the BI package (`bi-package.md`) has no `.pbix`; neither is evidence about those products.
* Claims stay consistent with the public case study (`productsignal/project.json` on `unified-portfolio`) and the resume bullets (`resume-bullets.md`).
