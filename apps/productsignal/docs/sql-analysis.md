# ProductSignal: SQL analysis (P06-ART-04)

**Every account, user, event and experiment here is synthetic.** Nothing is a claim about a real product, a real customer or a measured effect (P06-GOAL-12).
Dataset `ps-20261026-20261026` (seed 20261026, generator `dataset_hash` `12672f9c39402517083bbbbe9164cbefe3435361dff086eb0d9e16e60f9b66f8`), contract v1, code revision `fdf911a`; the figures describe the log as it stood at the as-of **2026-10-05T00:00:00Z** over the 26 weeks 2026-04-06 to 2026-10-04, and at the five logged analysis cutoffs of the experiment.
Three kinds of statement are kept apart throughout: **observed** (what the synthetic log shows, reconciled to `data/sql/reference_metrics.sql` and the Python domain), **constructed** (what the generator built in on purpose: the +8.00 point treatment effect, v2's sample-ratio mismatch, the INJ-10 instrumentation gap, and the four outcome-informed choices of section 10; a designed relationship that the SQL finds is a check on the generator, not a discovery) and **hypothetical** (what a real product team might gain; none is claimed and no figure below is one).
**The v1 lift:** published **+9.50 percentage points** (63 of 200 control against 82 of 200 treatment accounts, Newcombe 95% interval +0.000812 to +0.186863), **conclusive by a hair**: the lower bound is +0.000812, that is +0.08 points. The published figure sits inside the INJ-10 instrumentation gap: **published +9.50, drawn +11.50, +9.96 excluding the 11 gap accounts** (section 5).

**How to read it:** this file needs nothing installed. Every table between `<!-- BEGIN:... -->` markers is rendered from `artifacts/analysis-results.json` by `artifacts/render_sql_analysis.py` (no pasted figure is typed by hand) and `tests/test_artifacts.py` fails on drift. The queries are in `data/sql/analysis/`; `run_all.py` runs them and prints the reconciliation of section 9.

## Method
* **Scratch databases, never the shared one.** `prepare_snapshot.py` creates and resets databases named `artifacts06_*`, applies `data/migrations/0001_schema.sql` and `0002_constraints.sql` through `data/migrate.py` and loads `web/public/data` through `data/seed.py` (the domain's `validate_batch`, as `tests/test_generator.py` does): the entity files, the manifest's metric windows, **both** plans (v1 and v2) from the manifest's `analysis_plans`, dataset version `ps-20261026-20261026` and the generator's hash. A schema cannot stand in for a database: `0002_constraints.sql` looks constraint names up globally in `pg_constraint`. The golden fixture loads into its own scratch database.
* **`00_canonical_events.sql`** repeats the canonical-event common table expressions of `data/sql/reference_metrics.sql` (received by the cutoff, deduplicated with the earliest `received_at` winning, identity forward only, bots, internal accounts and unknown identities classified) with the cutoff as a column, at seven cutoffs: the four v1 runs (an-001 2026-05-18, an-002 2026-06-15, an-003 2026-07-13, an-004 2026-08-02 = planned_end), v2's run (an-005 2026-10-04), the as-of, and 2026-10-02T12:00Z (INJ-06's earlier read). It reads facts only: no stored result, verdict or status.
* **The cutoff rule (D-08):** a run at cutoff C sees only the facts that existed by C (assignments with `assigned_at <= C`, exposures with `exposed_at <= C`, subscriptions with `starts_at <= C`, events received by C). The unit is the account; the primary analysis is intention to treat over the assigned accounts that are mature (signup + 7 x 24 h <= C). Every interval added to a timestamp is hour-based (`make_interval(hours => 24 * n)`), so the result does not depend on the session TimeZone (R6-03).
* **Statistics in SQL.** Wilson and Newcombe bounds are exact `numeric` with square roots at 40 decimals, rounded once half up; the chi-square p-value is the Maclaurin series of erf in `numeric` at 90 decimals; the Student t quantile is read from `t_critical_975` (generated by Python, the one number SQL cannot derive); the Welch degrees of freedom are rounded down.
* **Calendars.** Day-7 retention is on the account's own IANA calendar (`timestamptz AT TIME ZONE tz`); the week of a cohort is the Monday of the local signup date; tracking weeks are UTC Mondays of `event_at`.
* **The Python side.** `data/sql/analysis/python_side.py` recomputes every result set from the published JSON rows with its own event canonicalisation, `zoneinfo`, `Fraction` and `Decimal` (no SQL, no ProductSignal domain import); `run_all.py` compares every cell (section 9, rows C). It shares only `data/sql/t_quantiles.csv` with the SQL.
* **Limits that apply to every table:** one draw of one synthetic world; every account is a segment-tagged synthetic account; unassigned accounts are not part of the experiment; segments are exploratory (P06-DOMAIN-06) and none is a decision basis.

## 10. What this analysis does not claim
* **No effect on any real product is claimed.** The treatment effect (+8.00 points designed) and v2's mismatch are constructed; the realised lift is one draw. "Conclusive by a hair" is a statement about one interval whose lower bound is +0.000812 and about a result inside a known instrumentation gap, not a recommendation to ship.
* **The generator made four choices after looking at earlier drafts of the data** (disclosed in the manifest): v1's randomisation was changed from a fair coin (223 / 177, p 0.021) to permuted blocks; `product_error` was changed from per-account to 8% per session after an unrealistic guardrail gap appeared; v2's enrolment start and the number of bounce visitors were tuned; the INJ-06 candidate window was widened. Everything else is reported as drawn, once.
* **Segments are exploratory** (P06-DOMAIN-06): plan, segment and timezone figures here are descriptions of the log, with no multiplicity correction and no decision basis; the site's Bonferroni family (k = 9) is not applied to these tables.
* **Excel and Power BI were not run**, and no `.pbix` exists; the workbook was recalculated by LibreOffice only (`workbook.md`, `bi-package.md`).
* **Five of the twelve injected defects live only in replay events** (INJ-01, 02, 03, 09 and 11: attempts through the API that the commands refuse and that leave no row in a snapshot, so no query can find them); the SQL finds the seven that live in the data (INJ-04, 05, 06, 07, 08, 10 and 12, see the E rows of section 9) and the adversarial tests carry the other five.
* **Runs created by a person do not exist here.** The five runs are seeded rows; no decision, guardrail review or audit row exists in the snapshot database (`decisions`, `guardrail_reviews` and `audit_events` are empty); those are produced by C09 and C10 through the API.
