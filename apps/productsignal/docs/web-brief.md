# ProductSignal — static site brief (Mode B)

Visual direction (P06-GOAL-03): a **product journey laboratory**. Warm white `#faf7f2` ground, cobalt `#1f3fbf`
(text `#1a339a`), violet `#6d4fc7` for the treatment variant, rule `#d9d4ca`, ink `#1b1b24`, muted `#6e6a7a`. Type: a
geometric sans for display with a humanist sans for reading and a mono for figures. Principal representations: the
**funnel** (visitors → signup → activation with sample sizes, control beside treatment) and the **cohort grid**
(signup week × day, maturity-aware, immature cells visibly blank). Must not resemble the other labs.

Routes under `/projects-site/labs/productsignal/`: `/` landing; `/overview/` journey overview (entry: visitors to
activation with sample sizes); `/funnel/` funnel explorer (entry: ordered events and exclusions); `/cohorts/` cohort
laboratory (entry: maturity-aware retention grid); `/tracking/` tracking quality; `/experiments/` registry;
`/result/` experiment result (entry: the golden case reproduced in the browser, then the snapshot experiment:
absolute effect with interval, SRM first, guardrails); `/population/` assigned vs exposed denominators; `/segments/`
exploratory segment analysis with its label and multiplicity caveat; `/decision/` decision memo (entry: evidence and
the inconclusive option); `/evidence/`; `/case-study/` (entry). Same routing, manifest, honesty, motion and Playwright
evidence rules as the other apps.
