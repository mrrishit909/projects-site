# RetentionLab — static site brief (Mode B)

Visual direction (P10-GOAL-03): a **customer intelligence lab**. Dark plum ground `#2a1630`, soft cream text
`#f4ecdf`, coral `#ef7a6a` (on cream `#a8402f`) for risk emphasis, rule `#4a2e52`, muted `#bfae9f`, a cool teal
`#7fc8b8` for calibration references. Type: a geometric sans for display, a humanist sans for reading, a mono for
figures. Principal representations: the **reliability curve with sample counts** and the **precision–recall at
capacity** chart with the contact budget beside it. Must not resemble the other labs.

Routes under `/projects-site/labs/retentionlab/`: `/` landing; `/overview/` retention overview (entry: mature
cohorts, the churn definition, censored counts); `/cohorts/` cohort explorer (plan and tenure context);
`/features/` feature evidence (point-in-time lineage, availability, arrived-late counts); `/models/` model
comparison (entry: random, rule and logistic on the held-out test with the golden case recomputed);
`/calibration/` calibration lab (entry: reliability curve with bin counts, Brier); `/capacity/` capacity planner
(entry: precision and recall at K, risk lift, contact budget, simulated); `/account/` account explanation (risk
factors without causal claims); `/queue/` outreach queue (synthetic draft actions, simulated); `/monitoring/`
(drift, maturity, scoring freshness); `/evidence/`; `/case-study/` (entry: model card, evaluation and intervention
limits). Same routing, manifest, honesty, motion and Playwright evidence rules as the other apps.
