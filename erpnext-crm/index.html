01The problem

A small distributor converts won opportunities into sales while keeping stock and the books consistent, without double posting.

Defining workflow: Receive ten units, win an opportunity, convert it to an order without retyping, fulfill three, invoice, take payment: seven units remain, receivables clear and debits equal credits; retrying any step never duplicates a movement or an entry.

02What I built

One of eleven independent CRM MVPs in a single monorepo, each with its own database, migrations, tests and review packet. This one is inspired by ERPNext and is called Fennel Distribution.

  • CRM, Sales, Inventory and Ledger modules with explicit service boundaries
  • Won opportunity to sales order, fulfillment at moving-average cost, invoice with flat tax, payments
  • Double-entry ledger whose balance and immutability are enforced by the database (deferred balance triggers, reversal instead of deletion)
  • Idempotent commands (Idempotency-Key), locked stock and invoice rows, integrity report, trial balance, customer balances

03How it was built

  1. 01

    Specify before building

    An acceptance document names the persona, the bounded scope, what is deferred, and ties every required feature to evidence (a test, an end-to-end journey or a recorded check).

  2. 02

    Model the database so the rules hold even if the code is wrong

    A Prisma schema plus a hand-written migration of CHECK constraints, composite foreign keys that pin every record to its workspace, and triggers. The tests try to break these from raw SQL.

  3. 03

    Build the distinguishing workflow through every layer

    CRM, Sales, Inventory and Ledger modules with explicit service boundaries

  4. 04

    Prove it in a fresh clone

    20 gate checks pass and 0 fail in a fresh clone of commit 55ab57c54c: 10 unit, 59 integration against a real PostgreSQL, 17 browser tests on the production build, container start, a live black-box check, restart persistence, backup and restore, and a production-dependency audit.

  5. 05

    Review it as an independent reader would

    A review packet lists what is implemented, what is measured, what was fixed on the way, and every known limit and release blocker, so nothing here is claimed beyond its evidence.

05What I'd do next

  • One currency (USD, integer cents), whole-unit quantities, one flat tax rate, moving-average costing
  • No purchasing, multi-currency, discounts, credit notes or period close; not statutory accounting software
  • All posting transactions of a workspace serialize on the journal counter (fine for a small distributor)
  • Not production-ready: no TLS, proxy or monitoring provisioned
