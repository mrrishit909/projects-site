The instrument

One register, four arrangements.

Thirty-two parts: a brass rail and cap, two frames, an engraved plate between green lacquer and marble, four pins, a lacquer base. Nothing on it is a count; every arrangement above is drawn from a project's real structure and labelled where it could be mistaken for data.

Drag with a mouse, or use the buttons; arrow keys turn it while a button has focus.

Materials brushed brass (champagne), racing-green lacquer, green and ivory marble, four steel-finish pins. Rendered live in WebGL from procedural geometry (open the lab).
